About CORAL

The Colorado Ophthalmology Research AI Lab (CORAL) focuses on developing cutting-edge artificial intelligence solutions for ophthalmology. Led by Dr. Jayashree Kalpathy-Cramer, our lab combines expertise in machine learning, medical imaging, and clinical ophthalmology to advance patient care and research.

Research Areas

AI in Ophthalmology

Developing advanced machine learning models for eye disease diagnosis and prognosis.

Medical Image Analysis

Creating innovative solutions for retinal image processing and analysis.

Clinical Translation

Bridging the gap between AI research and clinical practice.
