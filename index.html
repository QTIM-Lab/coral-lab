About CORAL

The Colorado Ophthalmology Research AI Lab (CORAL) at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus focuses on developing cutting-edge artificial intelligence solutions for ophthalmology. Led by Dr. Jayashree Kalpathy-Cramer, our lab combines expertise in machine learning, medical imaging, and clinical ophthalmology to advance patient care and research. We are part of the Department of Ophthalmology at the CU School of Medicine.

Research Areas

AI in Ophthalmology

Developing advanced machine learning models for eye disease diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment planning.

Medical Image Analysis

Creating innovative solutions for retinal image processing, analysis, and biomarker development.

Clinical Translation

Bridging the gap between AI research and clinical practice in partnership with CU Anschutz Medical Campus.
