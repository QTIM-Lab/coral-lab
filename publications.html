Featured Publication

A review of deep learning for brain tumor analysis in MRI

Felix J Dorfner, Jay B Patel, Jayashree Kalpathy-Cramer, Elizabeth R Gerstner, Christopher P Bridge

NPJ Precision Oncology, 2025

Recent progress in deep learning (DL) is producing a new generation of tools across numerous clinical applications. Within the analysis of brain tumors in magnetic resonance imaging, DL finds applications in tumor segmentation, quantification, and classification. It facilitates objective and reproducible measurements crucial for diagnosis, treatment planning, and disease monitoring. Furthermore, it holds the potential to pave the way for personalized medicine through the prediction of tumor type, grade, genetic mutations, and patient survival outcomes.
